# ADR-0007: Matcher plugin and common correspondence contract

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** not yet decided
- **Blocks:** WP-04 baselines; WP-05 channels; WP-06 learned challenger; WP-07 selection
- **Related decisions:** R-004

## Context

Classical baselines and the learned challenger must be comparable on identical inputs, masks, splits, and metrics. Neither an auxiliary channel nor a learned model may redefine the correspondence schema.
